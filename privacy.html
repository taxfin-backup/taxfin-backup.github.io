Privacy policy

This application is used only by its owner to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive using rclone.

What data is accessed

The application accesses Google Drive only to upload, list and update the owner's backup files. It does not access any other Google data.

How data is used and stored

Files are transferred directly between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. No data is sent to, stored by, or shared with any third party. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account under Security > Third-party apps & services.
